## What is not yet supported

## What would confirm propagation

1. Factor footprint and synchronous winner liquidation become elevated together across a broader cross-section.
2. Liquidity-absorption failure appears while losses spread beyond the detected cluster and technology sector.
3. Independent positioning data show continued long reduction across the affected book rather than rapid re-entry.

## What would invalidate the unwind thesis

1. Cluster losses reverse while turnover normalizes and absorption remains healthy.
2. Breadth stays healthy and stress remains confined to a few names without factor-wide propagation.
3. Earnings revisions and order data remain positive without a measurable valuation or cash-flow reprice.

## PM interpretation

The May 29 evidence warrants focused monitoring of long-side concentration and correlated-theme stress—not a completed DM crash, broad forced unwind, or confirmed fundamental deterioration. Next checks: whether losses and factor footprint propagate beyond the detected cluster, whether absorption fails, and whether independent positioning data show continued long reduction rather than rapid re-entry. Any read of the post-cutoff semiconductor selloff needs a refreshed structured snapshot and timestamp-matched evidence.
