# Reviewer checklist

Use this checklist for every completed annotation batch.

## Scope and timing

- [ ] The assessment date is `2026-05-29`.
- [ ] Every included source was public by `2026-05-29T16:00:00-04:00`.
- [ ] Date-only sources remain date precision; no time was invented.
- [ ] Post-cutoff retrospectives and the June chip selloff are excluded.
- [ ] The 66-day snapshot staleness is disclosed wherever “current” could be inferred.

## Evidence integrity

- [ ] Evidence IDs are unique.
- [ ] Titles, sources, and locators match the referenced pages.
- [ ] Stored passages are minimal and faithful.
- [ ] Passage hashes match `candidate_evidence.json`.
- [ ] No full copyrighted article or long excerpt is stored.
- [ ] Second-hand positioning data are identified as such.

## Mechanism discipline

- [ ] DM prior panic, recovery, and loser-leg rebound are assessed separately.
- [ ] Crowding is not treated as proof of forced liquidation.
- [ ] Public turnover/liquidity proxies are not described as dealer inventory.
- [ ] Strong fundamentals and capex pressure are both considered.
- [ ] The statistical cluster is not called a validated semiconductor theme.
- [ ] Sector repricing and factor propagation remain explicitly unresolved where evidence is insufficient.
- [ ] Contradicting evidence is retained rather than suppressed.

## PM safety

- [ ] No composite probability was created.
- [ ] No causal certainty is asserted.
- [ ] No trade, security, sizing, or execution instruction appears.
- [ ] Structured values are copied without recalculation or manual adjustment.
- [ ] Missing evidence is explicit.
- [ ] Conclusions remain conditional and bounded to the frozen date.
